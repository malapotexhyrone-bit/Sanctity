ABOUT US

Sanctity is a student-led advocacy website that promotes awareness of human dignity through digital art and reflection. It was created as a space where stories, creativity, and values come together to encourage respect, empathy, and understanding in everyday life.

Behind this project is Project Dignidad, a group of students from the University of La Salette committed to promoting the value of every human person. Inspired by Salettinian ideals, the team believes that dignity is not something earned, but something that every individual already possesses.

OUR MISSION

Our mission is to inspire people, especially students, to recognize and respect human dignity in both physical and digital spaces. Through art and storytelling, we aim to encourage kindness, inclusivity, and responsible behavior.

OUR VISION

We envision a community where every person feels seen, heard, and valued—regardless of their background, identity, or circumstances.

WHAT WE DO

  • Create and share digital artworks that reflect real-life experiences
  • Promote awareness of respect, empathy, and inclusivity
  • Encourage reflection on everyday actions and choices
  • Provide a platform for student voices and perspectives

CORE VALUES

  • Dignity — Every person has inherent worth
  • Empathy — We listen before we judge
  • Respect — We treat others as we want to be treated
  • Inclusivity — No one is left behind
